Retain these credits when reusing the demonstration.

The grayscale SVG depth maps and illustrated preview are original Tampa Curated artwork, MIT. They are hand-painted **depth estimates**, not measured reconstructions. They intentionally simplify foliage, windows, sky and ground. Uploaded photographs, depth maps and reconstructions remain the visitor's responsibility and are never uploaded or added to exports.

No Gaussian-splat reconstruction is bundled. In Mode B the framed image is captured from the visitor's own loaded scene, preserving a corresponding viewpoint.
