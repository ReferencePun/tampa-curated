## A neighborhood, not a checklist

Brick streets, layered histories, and a city that comes alive after dark. Our approach to Ybor City starts with time on the street: looking closely, listening, and leaving room for a detour.

This is a sample neighborhood introduction showing how future field notes will read. Before publication, our editors will add firsthand observations, original photographs, and conversations with the people who live and work here.

## The way we look

A good neighborhood story follows the small details. The doorway you have passed a hundred times. A workshop with its lights still on. The public place where a private ritual becomes part of city life.
