## A practice in progress

Mara Ellis is a fictional character created to demonstrate the People to Watch format. This imagined visual artist profile explores a theme we would like to investigate through real interviews: working between image, memory, and the things we choose to keep.

In a published profile, this space will hold a biography drawn from a recorded conversation, a studio visit, and fact-checked details about the subject’s work.

## The conversation

**What are you making space for?**

This is a sample interview prompt. An approved response from the real subject will appear here; no invented quotation is attributed to a real person.

**What does the city need more of?**

Another opening for a thoughtful answer. We will edit for clarity while preserving the subject’s voice.

**Where should we look next?**

The finished profile will connect to verified projects, exhibitions, collaborators, and the subject’s own channels.
