Tampa is a city best understood at street level. Beyond the familiar silhouette, there is another way to look: through the windows of a small studio, across a crowded table, or down a side street you have never taken.

This opening essay sets out the lens of Tampa Curated. We are interested in the people making things, the rooms bringing people together, and the ideas that give a place its character.

## A city is a collection of small things

The memorable places are rarely memorable for only one reason. There is the room itself, of course. But there is also the person who opens it every morning, the music playing quietly in the corner, and the way one encounter leads to another.

Our future reporting will begin with those details. We will spend time in the places we cover, ask questions that take more than a sound bite to answer, and distinguish a considered recommendation from an advertisement.

## Keep looking

This first edition is a design demonstration, a picture of the publication we want to make. The reporting is still ahead. The invitation is simple: pay attention to your city, and leave a little space to be surprised.
