Independence is visible in the details: a menu edited down to what matters, a chair repaired instead of replaced, a shop window arranged by the person who will greet you inside.

This sample story sketches our approach to covering small businesses. The point is to understand the work, not just photograph the finished result.

## Behind the front door

A future feature will follow one business from the first idea to an ordinary working day. We will ask about decisions, compromises, collaborations, and the less photogenic parts of building something that lasts.

## Local is a relationship

The most interesting question is what a business gives back to the place around it. Does it create room for other makers? Does it know its neighbors? What can the rest of the city learn from the way it works?

These are editorial questions for future reporting. The businesses shown in the demonstration directory are fictional, and no endorsement is implied.
