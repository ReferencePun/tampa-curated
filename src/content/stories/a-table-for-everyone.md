Some spaces are most valuable for what they allow: a quiet hour, an accidental conversation, a familiar face across the counter. The everyday café is one of them.

This sample essay shows how a food story can reach beyond a menu. The real reporting will explore the labor, routines, and relationships behind the room.

## The daily ritual

We want to know what people return for and what the people behind the counter notice. The answers will come from visits and interviews, not assumptions.
