A creative scene is made in conversation. One person makes an object; another finds a place to show it. A friend brings a friend. Eventually, something that began as an individual practice starts to feel like a shared language.

This sample story introduces the intention behind People to Watch. We want to follow work while it is still taking shape, with room for uncertainty and experiments.

## In the studio

The real profiles will begin with a visit and an open conversation. We will publish original portraits, thoughtful interviews, and links that let readers discover the work for themselves.

## The names to know

The profiles in this first design edition are explicitly fictional. Their names, professions, and biographies demonstrate the format and must be replaced with approved interviews before they become real editorial coverage.
