There is a pleasure in changing a familiar route. Turn a block earlier. Walk a little farther. Look at the upper floors as well as the storefronts.

Our neighborhood guides are designed around that spirit. They will pair practical, verified details with the less measurable things that make a place itself.

## Follow your attention

In the published edition, each guide will connect independent businesses, local voices, and stories from the surrounding streets. It will be a starting point for curiosity rather than a ranking.

## Leave room for a detour

The six guide pages in this demonstration show how the publication fits together. Their associated businesses are fictional examples, and the images are labeled where they are illustrative rather than neighborhood-specific.
